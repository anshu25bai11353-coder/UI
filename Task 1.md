## Task 1: Product Thinking  
Pick your favorite website or app. If you were part of its development team, what feature would you
introduce or remove, and why?

### Favourite App: Instagram

If I was part of the Instagram team, I would add a Study Mode feature.

When a user turns on Study Mode, Reels and other distracting videos will be hidden for a fixed time, like 30 minutes or 1 hour. Only messages and important notifications will be shown.

### Why?

Many students open Instagram for a few minutes but keep watching Reels for a long time. Study Mode will help them stay focused on their studies and reduce screen time. After the study time is over, they can use Instagram normally again.

I think this feature will help students use Instagram in a better and healthier way while still enjoying the app.
