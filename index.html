About

What is Hackathon 2026?

A 48-hour innovation marathon where developers, designers, and entrepreneurs come together to build impactful solutions.

Hackathon 2026 is more than just a competition — it's a launchpad for ideas that matter. Whether you're a seasoned developer or a first-time hacker, you'll find mentorship, resources, and a community that pushes you to think bigger.

From AI and climate tech to health and education, we're tackling the most pressing challenges of our time. No idea is too bold, and no team is too small.
